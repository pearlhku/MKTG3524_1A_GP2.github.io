A few little things to know.

Can I choose a blind box design?

Single blind boxes contain a random design. Set contents differ by series, and a hidden edition is not guaranteed. Choose a named design from the Hong Kong and Macau Clicker collection if you prefer to know what you will receive.

Are these toys for small children?

The brand specifies ages 14 and up. They are not for small children or chewing.

Where can I place a real order?

This is a student website with a demonstration cart. Use the official store links for current prices, stock, shipping and real orders. Product-specific dispatch notices take precedence over general shipping information.
